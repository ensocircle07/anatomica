# ADR-0002: Merge syndesmology into "Arthrology (joints and ligaments)"

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-06

## Context
The specification listed syndesmology as a separate discipline. In classical terminology, syndesmology is the study of joints and ligaments, which is the same subject matter as arthrology. Two sections would duplicate ligament content and violate the single-record principle.

## Decision
One systematic section, **"Arthrology (joints and ligaments)"**. Ligaments are their own entities (`ligament`) linked to joints and bones. "Syndesmology" is a search synonym for the section.

## Consequences
- No duplicated ligament content.
- Users searching for "syndesmology" still reach the section.
